Privacy & cookies

How this academic project website handles visitor information.

Privacy information

This is a static academic website. It does not use advertising, analytics, user accounts, contact forms, or first-party profiling cookies.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set first-party cookies and does not store preferences or other information in the visitor’s browser. As a result, no cookie-consent banner is required for the site’s own functionality.

External services

Links to GitHub, Hugging Face, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, X, institutional websites, publishers, conference websites, and other research resources open external services governed by their respective privacy policies. These services are contacted only when a visitor follows a link to them.

Downloads

Publications, BibTeX files, slides, and other research materials may be downloaded directly from this website or from external services. The website does not use those downloads to build visitor profiles.

Email and hosting

Email links open the visitor’s configured email application; no message is sent through this website. The hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address, requested page, timestamp, and browser information, for security and service operation.

Contact
